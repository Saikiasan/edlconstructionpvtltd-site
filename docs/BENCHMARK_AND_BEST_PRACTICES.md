## 3. Implemented Design System Specifications

### 🎨 Color Hierarchy
* **Obsidian Blueprint (`#07090E`)**: Primary background representing architectural night-mode and executive studios.
* **Navy Drafting Slate (`#09101E`)**: Secondary container background for CAD schematics and technical cards.
* **Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`)**: Primary brand accent representing premium craftsmanship, precision brass, and luxury finishings.
* **Polished Amber Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: Interactive hover and active glow states.
* **Emerald Precision (`#10B981`)**: Approved milestone badges, completed telemetry, and safety indicators.
* **Warm Alabaster (`#FAF9F6`)**: Light-mode daytime sections providing crisp typographic contrast.

### 📐 Typography Matrix
* **Headings**: *Playfair Display* & *Cinzel* for timeless corporate dignity.
* **Brand / Display**: *Syne (700/800)* for structural modernity and bold logo identity.
* **Technical Telemetry**: *JetBrains Mono* for coordinates, tolerances, concrete grades, and cost figures.
* **Body Copy**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* for high-legibility mobile reading.
